01 OUR STORY

More than a gift.
A lasting impression.

Longway Import & Export is a South African corporate gifting company focused on creating thoughtful, personalised gifting experiences for businesses and their people.

From the first idea to the final delivery, we work closely with our clients to create gifts that reflect their brand, their values and the relationships they want to celebrate.

Our ambition extends beyond corporate gifting. We are building toward becoming a trusted South Africa–Africa trading partner.

OUR VISION

From South Africa, for Africa.

We believe meaningful business relationships are built through thoughtful experiences. Our long-term vision is to connect businesses across Africa through reliable sourcing, personalised gifting and exceptional service.
